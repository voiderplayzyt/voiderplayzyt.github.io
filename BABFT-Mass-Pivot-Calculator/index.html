Height / 3D rule

For a horizontal pivot with gravity acting vertically, use the perpendicular horizontal lever arm. Moving a counterweight straight up/down does not change its balancing moment if its lever arm stays the same.

For another rotation axis, use the perpendicular distance from the object's center of mass to that axis.
