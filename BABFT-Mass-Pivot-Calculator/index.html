⑤ Counterweight Shape / Pivot View

Torque balance: Σ(Mᵢ × Xᵢ) = Beam Mass × Beam Distance
Block volume: Vᵢ = X-size × Y-size × Z-size
Block mass: Mᵢ = Densityᵢ × Vᵢ
Counterweight X-COM: Σ(Mᵢ × Xᵢ) ÷ ΣMᵢ
Counterweight Y-COM: Σ(Mᵢ × Yᵢ) ÷ ΣMᵢ
Required uniform density: (Beam Mass × Beam Distance) ÷ Σ(Vᵢ × |Xᵢ|)
