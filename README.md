## 🧰 Projects

### ⚖️ BABFT Mass & Pivot Calculator

A physics helper for planning counterweights in **Build A Boat For Treasure (BABFT)**. Add multiple blocks—even an irregular layout—and see how their mass and position affect balance.

- Find the counterweight mass or uniform density needed to balance a beam
- Choose one shared density or use each block’s own density
- View volume, center of mass, torque, balance ratio, and mass to add or remove
- Calculate the combined center of mass for two objects
- Copy a summary of your calculation

**[Open the calculator](https://voiderplayzyt.github.io/BABFT-Mass-Pivot-Calculator/)** · [View its files](https://github.com/voiderplayzyt/voiderplayzyt.github.io/tree/main/BABFT-Mass-Pivot-Calculator)

### 🔤 Word & Number Guesser

A guessing game and clue tracker with separate **word** and **number** modes. Choose a topic, generate a secret, then use feedback from each guess to narrow down the answer.

- Pick a secret from the selected topic
- Track exact green, yellow, and red feedback—or enter green/yellow totals
- Review previous guesses and possible word candidates
- Explore word topics, including singular and plural nouns and other noun categories

**[Play the game](https://voiderplayzyt.github.io/Word-Number-Guesser/)** · [View its files](https://github.com/voiderplayzyt/voiderplayzyt.github.io/tree/main/Word-Number-Guesser)
